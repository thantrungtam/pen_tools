About Cipher Tools

A collection of encryption, encoding, and decoding tools built with Django.

What is Cipher Tools?

Cipher Tools is a free online platform that provides a collection of utilities for encoding, decoding, encrypting, and decrypting data. Our tools are designed to be simple and easy to use, with an intuitive interface that makes it easy to convert your data between different formats.

All of our tools run directly in your browser - we don't send your data to our servers for processing, ensuring your information remains private and secure.

Our Tools

We offer a variety of tools including:

  • URL Encoder/Decoder - Convert URLs to a web-safe format and back
  • Base64 Encoder/Decoder - Convert binary data to a text format and back
  • MD5 Hash Generator - Generate an MD5 hash from your input text
  • HTML Encoder/Decoder - Convert text with HTML special characters
  • Caesar Cipher - Encrypt and decrypt using the classic Caesar shift cipher
